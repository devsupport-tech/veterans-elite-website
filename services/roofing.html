FAQ

Roofing Questions

How long does a new roof installation take?

Most residential roof installations are completed in 1-3 days depending on the size of your home and the roofing material chosen. Commercial projects may take longer. We provide a detailed timeline during your free consultation.

What types of roofing materials do you work with?

We install and repair all major roofing materials including asphalt shingles, tile, metal, flat roofing systems, and specialty materials. We help you choose the best option for your property, budget, and San Diego's climate.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes, we offer complimentary roof inspections. Our licensed team will assess your roof's condition, identify any issues, and provide an honest recommendation with a detailed written estimate.

Does insurance cover storm damage to my roof?

Most homeowner's insurance policies cover storm damage to roofs. We work directly with insurance companies and can assist with the claims process to help you get the coverage you deserve.
