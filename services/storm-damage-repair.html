FAQ

Storm Damage Questions

How quickly can you respond to storm damage?

We maintain 24/7 storm response teams throughout San Diego County. During major storm events, we prioritize emergency board-up and tarping to prevent further damage, typically arriving within 60-90 minutes.

Does insurance cover storm damage?

Most homeowner's insurance covers wind and rain damage. However, flood damage from rising water typically requires separate flood insurance. We help document damage and work with your insurance to maximize coverage.

What should I do after a storm damages my property?

First, ensure everyone is safe. Document visible damage with photos. Don't make permanent repairs before your insurance adjuster sees the damage, but do take steps to prevent further damage. Call us for emergency tarping and board-up.

Can storm water cause mold?

Yes, mold can begin growing within 24-48 hours of water intrusion. That's why rapid water extraction and professional drying is critical after storm damage. We ensure complete drying to prevent mold growth.
