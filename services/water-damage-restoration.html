FAQ

Water Damage Restoration Questions

How quickly can you respond to water damage in Del Mar?

Veterans Elite Contractors offers 60-minute emergency response throughout Del Mar and San Diego County. Our teams are available 24/7/365, including holidays. We understand that water damage worsens by the minute, so we prioritize rapid response to minimize damage and restoration costs.

What should I do immediately after discovering water damage?

First, ensure safety by turning off electricity if water is near outlets. Stop the water source if possible (shut off main valve for burst pipes). Move valuable items to dry areas, but don't enter rooms with standing water if electricity is on. Call us immediately at (858) 555-0123.

Does insurance cover water damage restoration?

Most homeowner's insurance policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as burst pipes or appliance failures. Flood damage from external sources typically requires separate flood insurance. We work directly with all major insurance companies and can bill them directly.

How long does water damage restoration take?

The timeline depends on the extent of damage. Water extraction typically takes 1-2 days. Structural drying usually requires 3-5 days with professional equipment. Complete restoration, including repairs and reconstruction, can take 1-2 weeks for minor damage or several weeks for extensive damage.

Can water damage cause mold growth?

Yes, mold can begin growing within 24-48 hours of water exposure. This is why rapid response is critical. We use professional-grade dehumidifiers and air movers to thoroughly dry all affected areas, preventing mold growth. We also offer mold remediation if mold has already developed.

Do you handle the insurance claim process?

Yes! We work directly with all major insurance companies. We document all damage thoroughly, provide detailed estimates, and can bill your insurance directly. Our team helps streamline the claims process to minimize your stress during an already difficult time.
