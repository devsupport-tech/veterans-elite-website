Our Process

Custom Glass Installation Process

From design to installation, every detail is handled with precision.

1

Consultation & Measurement

We visit your space to discuss your vision, take precise measurements, and recommend the best glass type, thickness, and hardware for your application.

2

Design & Selection

Choose from a range of glass types (clear, frosted, tinted, patterned), hardware finishes (chrome, brushed nickel, matte black), and configurations.

3

Custom Fabrication

Your glass is precision-cut and fabricated to exact specifications, with all edges polished and holes drilled for hardware mounting.

4

Professional Installation

Our experienced installers carefully mount and secure your glass, ensuring perfect alignment, watertight seals, and smooth operation.

5

Final Inspection & Care Guide

We inspect every detail, clean the installation, and provide care instructions to keep your glass looking pristine for years to come.

FAQ

Custom Glass Questions

How long does a custom glass installation take?

Most residential installations like shower enclosures are completed in 1-2 days. Custom projects requiring fabrication may take 2-3 weeks from measurement to installation. We provide detailed timelines during your consultation.

What types of glass do you offer?

We offer clear, frosted, tinted, low-iron, patterned, and tempered glass options. All shower and safety applications use tempered safety glass that meets building code requirements.

Do you offer frameless shower enclosures?

Yes, frameless shower enclosures are one of our most popular services. We custom-measure and fabricate each enclosure for a perfect fit, using heavy-duty tempered glass and premium hardware.

Can you replace broken glass quickly?

For standard sizes, we can often complete replacements within a few days. Custom pieces may require fabrication time. Contact us for an assessment and we'll provide the fastest solution possible.
