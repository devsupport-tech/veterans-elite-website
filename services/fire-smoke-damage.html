Complete Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration

Fire damage extends far beyond the flames. Smoke, soot, and the water used to extinguish fires can cause extensive secondary damage throughout your property. Without immediate professional intervention, acidic soot residue permanently etches into surfaces, and smoke odor penetrates deep into materials. Veterans Elite Contractors provides comprehensive fire damage restoration to return your home or business to pre-loss condition.

Our Fire Damage Services Include:

Emergency Board-Up & Tarping

Immediate property securing to protect against weather, theft, and further damage while restoration planning begins.

Soot & Smoke Removal

Professional cleaning of all surfaces using specialized techniques for different soot types (wet, dry, protein) before permanent staining occurs.

Water Extraction & Drying

Firefighting water and sprinkler systems often cause significant water damage. We extract water and dry structures to prevent mold growth.

Odor Elimination

FAQ

Fire Damage Restoration Questions

How soon should I start fire damage restoration?

Immediately. Soot and smoke residue begin causing permanent damage within hours. Acidic soot etches into surfaces, and smoke odor penetrates deeper over time. We respond 24/7 to begin mitigation as soon as the fire department clears your property.

Does homeowner's insurance cover fire damage?

Yes, most policies cover fire damage restoration including smoke damage, water damage from firefighting, and temporary living expenses. We work directly with all major insurance companies and handle the claims process for you.

Can smoke odor be completely removed?

Yes, with professional equipment and techniques. We use thermal fogging, ozone treatment, hydroxyl generators, and air scrubbers to neutralize smoke odor at the molecular level. Our technicians eliminate odors, not just mask them.

How long does fire damage restoration take?

Timeline varies based on damage extent. Minor smoke damage may take 1-2 weeks. Major structural damage requiring reconstruction can take several months. We provide detailed timelines during assessment and keep you informed throughout.
