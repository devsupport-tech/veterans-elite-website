FAQ

Asphalt Paving Questions

How long does a new asphalt driveway last?

A properly installed asphalt driveway typically lasts 15-20 years with regular maintenance. Sealcoating every 2-3 years can extend its lifespan significantly and keep it looking fresh.

How long before I can drive on new asphalt?

New asphalt typically needs 24-48 hours before light vehicle traffic and 5-7 days before heavy vehicles. Curing times can vary based on temperature and thickness. We provide specific guidance for your project.

What is sealcoating and do I need it?

Sealcoating is a protective layer applied over asphalt that shields it from UV rays, water, oil, and chemical damage. We recommend sealcoating every 2-3 years to extend your pavement's life and maintain its appearance.

Can you repair just a section of my parking lot?

Yes, we offer spot repairs, patching, and overlay services that address problem areas without requiring a full replacement. We assess the overall condition and recommend the most cost-effective solution.
