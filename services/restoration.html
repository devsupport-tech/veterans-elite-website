Full-Service Property Restoration

Whether it's a burst pipe, a house fire, hidden mold, or storm damage, Veterans Elite Contractors provides end-to-end restoration services to bring your property back to pre-loss condition. We handle everything from emergency mitigation and cleanup to complete reconstruction — and we work directly with your insurance company to streamline the claims process.

Our Restoration Services:

Water Damage Restoration

Emergency water extraction, structural drying, and complete restoration from floods, burst pipes, sewage backups, and appliance failures. Fast response to minimize damage and prevent mold growth.

Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration

Complete fire damage restoration including soot removal, smoke odor elimination, water extraction from firefighting, and structural reconstruction. Emergency board-up and content pack-out services available.

Mold Remediation

Professional mold inspection, testing, containment, and removal. We eliminate mold at the source and treat affected areas to prevent regrowth, restoring healthy indoor air quality to your property.

Storm Damage Repair

Emergency response for wind, rain, hail, and flood damage. We secure your property, assess the damage, and provide complete restoration from roof repairs to interior rebuilding.

Reconstruction & Rebuilding

Complete structural reconstruction after any type of damage. From framing and drywall to flooring, painting, and finishing — we restore your property to pre-loss condition or better.
