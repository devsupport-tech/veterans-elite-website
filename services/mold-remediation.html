Professional Mold Remediation Services

Mold can grow anywhere moisture is present - often hidden behind walls, under floors, or in HVAC systems. Beyond causing structural damage, mold releases spores that trigger allergies, asthma, respiratory issues, and other health problems. Veterans Elite Contractors provides comprehensive mold remediation following strict EPA and IICRC guidelines to safely eliminate mold and restore healthy indoor air quality.

Our Mold Remediation Services:

Mold Inspection & Testing

Comprehensive visual inspection plus air quality and surface sampling to identify mold types, extent of growth, and moisture sources causing the problem.

Containment & Protection

Engineering controls including negative air pressure, physical barriers, and HEPA filtration to prevent spore spread during remediation.

Safe Mold Removal

Physical removal of mold-contaminated materials using proper PPE and disposal methods. HEPA vacuuming and antimicrobial treatment of affected surfaces.

Air Quality Restoration

HEPA air scrubbers and negative air machines remove airborne spores. Post-remediation testing confirms indoor air quality meets safe standards.

Prevention & Moisture Control

Identify and address moisture sources causing mold. Install dehumidification, improve ventilation, and apply preventive treatments to stop recurrence.

Health Risks of Mold Exposure

Mold exposure can cause a range of health issues, particularly for those with allergies, asthma, or compromised immune systems. Common symptoms include:

  • Respiratory issues and coughing
  • Nasal congestion and sneezing
  • Eye irritation and redness
  • Skin rashes and irritation
  • Headaches and fatigue
  • Aggravated asthma symptoms

FAQ

Mold Remediation Questions

How do I know if I have a mold problem?

Common signs include musty odors, visible mold growth, water stains, peeling paint, and health symptoms that improve when you leave the building. If you've had water damage or high humidity, mold may be growing hidden behind walls or under floors.

Is black mold dangerous?

While "black mold" (Stachybotrys chartarum) produces mycotoxins, all mold types should be professionally removed. Any mold can trigger health issues. The color doesn't determine toxicity - proper testing identifies mold types.

How long does mold remediation take?

Small areas typically take 1-3 days. Larger infestations may take 1-2 weeks. Timeline depends on extent of growth and whether structural repairs are needed. We provide detailed timelines after inspection.

Does insurance cover mold remediation?

Coverage varies by policy. Mold resulting from a covered event (like sudden water damage) is often covered. Mold from maintenance issues may not be. We help document and submit claims to maximize your coverage.
