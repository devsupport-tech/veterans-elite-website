Our Process

Epoxy Flooring Installation Process

Professional preparation and application for a flawless, long-lasting finish.

1

Consultation & Assessment

We evaluate your space, discuss your needs, and recommend the best epoxy system for your application, traffic level, and aesthetic preferences.

2

Surface Preparation

Thorough diamond grinding, crack repair, and moisture testing to create the ideal bonding surface. Proper prep is the foundation of a lasting epoxy floor.

3

Primer & Base Coat

Application of a high-adhesion primer followed by the epoxy base coat, building up layers for maximum durability and coverage.

4

Decorative Layer & Topcoat

Application of your chosen decorative finish (metallic, flake, solid color) followed by a UV-stable polyurethane or polyaspartic topcoat for protection and shine.

5

Curing & Final Walkthrough

Proper curing time ensures maximum hardness and chemical resistance. We complete a final walkthrough to ensure your complete satisfaction.

FAQ

Epoxy Flooring Questions

How long does epoxy flooring last?

Professionally installed epoxy flooring typically lasts 10-20 years or more depending on traffic levels and maintenance. Commercial and industrial applications with proper care can exceed 20 years.

How long does installation take?

A standard residential garage takes 2-3 days including surface preparation, application, and curing time. Larger commercial projects may take longer. We provide detailed timelines during your consultation.

Can epoxy be applied over existing concrete?

Yes, epoxy can be applied over most existing concrete surfaces. Our team thoroughly prepares the surface through diamond grinding and repairs to ensure optimal adhesion and a flawless finish.

Is epoxy flooring slippery when wet?

Standard epoxy can be slippery when wet, but we offer anti-slip additives and textured finishes (like flake systems) that provide excellent traction. We recommend these options for garages, kitchens, and commercial spaces.
